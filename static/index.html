End-to-End Pipeline Architecture

1. Data Ingestion: Kaggle Fake & Real News Dataset (~44.8k articles) merged & labeled (0=Real, 1=Fake).

2. Feature Fusion: Concatenates Title + Body text into single title_text sequence.

3. Advanced Preprocessing: Lowercasing, URL removal, non-alphanumeric strip, NLTK Lemmatizer, Stopwords filter.

4. Vectorization: TF-IDF Vectorizer with unigrams + bigrams (10,000 max features, sublinear TF scaling).

5. Model Training: ComplementNB model with GridSearchCV tuning over hyperparameter α ∈ [0.01, 5.0].

6. Robustness Evaluation: 10% Noise injection pipeline simulating character swaps, omissions, and word drops.
